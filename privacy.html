Website enquiries

This starter website does not submit information to a server-side contact form. Selecting a design opens an email draft in the visitor’s email application addressed to premiertechnologies.pk@gmail.com. The visitor chooses whether to send it.

Local preferences

The site stores the visitor’s Light/Dark theme choice in the browser’s local storage. It does not use that preference to identify the visitor.

Third-party resources

The page may load fonts from Google Fonts. This can cause the browser to connect to Google. Remove the Google Fonts links from index.html if you prefer to avoid this external request.

Hosting and updates

Hosting providers may process technical request data under their own policies. Review and update this policy to reflect the final hosting provider, any analytics, forms, cookies, and actual business practices before publication.
